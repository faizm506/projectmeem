What we collect

Account details (username, email, phone), delivery addresses, order history and messages you send us. Sellers also give shop details.

How we use it

To run your account, process orders, send order emails, prevent fraud and improve MEEM. We share a buyer's delivery details with the seller of the items they bought, and with delivery partners.

Payments

Card and bank details are handled by our payment provider. MEEM does not store them.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data by emailing us. Some records, such as orders and the donation ledger, must be kept for legal and accounting reasons.

Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you signed in and remember your cart.
