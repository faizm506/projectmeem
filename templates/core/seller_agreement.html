Policy

Seller Agreement.

Draft text. Have a lawyer in your country review and complete it before you launch.

Who can sell

Muslim-owned businesses and makers whose shop MEEM has approved. We may refuse or suspend any shop.

Your products

You promise your listings are accurate, legal, halal-aligned where claimed, and that you have the right to sell them.

Fees and donations

MEEM charges a commission on each item sold, shown in your dashboard earnings. MEEM sets aside the donation from its own commission, so your earning is not reduced by it.

Shipping

You ship paid orders promptly, add a tracking number, and mark items delivered.

Payouts

Earnings for delivered, non-refunded orders become available for payout. In this first version, MEEM pays sellers manually and records each payout.

Refunds and disputes

If a refund is approved, the earning from that order is removed from your balance.

Ending the agreement

Either side may end it at any time. Open orders must still be fulfilled.
