Our promise

If something is wrong with your order, tell us from the order page using "Report a problem".

When you can get a refund

  • The item never arrived.
  • The item is damaged or not as described.
  • You were charged twice.

How it works

We review your report and speak with the seller. If a refund is approved, the order is marked refunded and the money returns to your original payment method. This can take several business days. The donation set aside from a refunded order is reversed in our ledger.

Change of mind

Sellers may set their own return rules for change-of-mind returns. Check the product page or contact the seller.
